Website Privacy Notice

Effective Date: 9/25/2026

Brave Horizon Institute, LLC (“Brave Horizon Institute,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting information you provide when visiting or interacting with our website.

This Website Privacy Notice explains the types of information that may be collected through www.bravehorizoninstitute.com, how that information may be used and protected, and the choices available to you.

This Website Privacy Notice applies to information collected through our public website. Information collected as part of your psychotherapy or other health care services may be subject to additional privacy protections and is addressed separately in our Notice of Privacy Practices.

1. Information We May Collect

Information You Provide Voluntarily

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide through the website or when contacting Brave Horizon Institute, such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Information submitted through a contact or consultation request form
  • Scheduling information
  • Information you provide when requesting services or information; and
  • Other information you choose to provide.

Please do not submit detailed clinical information, trauma histories, Social Security numbers, financial account information, or other highly sensitive information through a general website contact form unless specifically instructed to use a secure method.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by our website, hosting provider, security services, analytics services, or other technologies used to operate the site.

This information may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Date and time of access
  • Pages viewed
  • Referring website or link
  • General geographic information derived from an IP address; and
  • Information about how visitors interact with the website.

This information may be used to maintain website security, troubleshoot technical issues, understand website usage, and improve the website and user experience.

2. Cookies And Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies necessary to operate the website, remember preferences, maintain security, understand website performance, or provide certain website features.

Depending on the services used on the website, third-party service providers may also use cookies or similar technologies.

You may be able to limit or block cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect how some website features function.

Because Brave Horizon Institute provides health care services, we take particular care when evaluating analytics, advertising, pixels, session-replay technologies, and similar tracking tools that could potentially receive information related to an individual's health care interests or services.

We do not intend to use website tracking technologies to improperly disclose protected health information.

3. How We May Use Information

Information collected through the website may be used to:

  • Respond to questions or requests
  • Respond to requests for a prospective-client consultation
  • Communicate regarding services
  • Facilitate scheduling when appropriate
  • Provide requested information or resources
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the website
  • Detect or prevent misuse, fraud, or security threats
  • Understand general website usage
  • Comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements; and
  • Carry out other purposes disclosed to you when information is collected.

Providing information through the website does not guarantee that Brave Horizon Institute will accept you as a client.

4. Prospective Clients And Therapist-Client Relationship

Visiting this website, submitting a contact form, requesting a consultation, sending an email, or otherwise contacting Brave Horizon Institute does not by itself establish a therapist-client relationship.

A therapist-client relationship is established only after Brave Horizon Institute has determined that services are appropriate, applicable intake and informed-consent requirements have been completed, and treatment has been formally initiated.

Please do not use the website, general email, contact forms, or consultation requests for emergencies or urgent mental health needs.

5. Missouri Service Area

Brandi Gillam, LPC, provides psychotherapy pursuant to her professional licensure in the State of Missouri.

Psychotherapy services through Brave Horizon Institute are currently available only when the client is physically located in Missouri at the time services are provided, unless the Provider has separate lawful authority to provide services in another jurisdiction.

The availability of this website outside Missouri does not represent an offer to provide psychotherapy in another state or jurisdiction.

Educational materials, general information, professional training, consulting, or other non-clinical services available through Brave Horizon Institute may be subject to different geographic limitations.

6. Health Information And Hipaa

Information obtained in connection with health care services may constitute Protected Health Information (“PHI”) and may be governed by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (“HIPAA”) and other applicable privacy laws.

Our use and disclosure of PHI is addressed in Brave Horizon Institute's Notice of Privacy Practices.

If there is a conflict between this Website Privacy Notice and the Notice of Privacy Practices concerning PHI, the Notice of Privacy Practices and applicable law will govern.

A website privacy notice is not a HIPAA authorization and does not authorize disclosure of PHI that would otherwise require a valid authorization or other legal basis.

7. Contact Forms, Email, And Electronic Communication

Information transmitted over the internet carries inherent privacy and security risks.

Unless a communication method is specifically identified as secure, you should not assume that a general website contact form, ordinary email, text message, or social-media message provides the same privacy protections as a secure client portal or other approved health care communication system.

Please limit information submitted through general website inquiries to what is reasonably necessary for us to respond to your request.

Existing clients should use the communication methods established by Brave Horizon Institute for clinical and administrative matters.

8. Third-Party Service Providers

Brave Horizon Institute may use third-party companies or service providers to support the operation of the website and practice.

Depending on the services implemented, these providers may include:

  • Website hosting providers
  • Domain providers
  • Website security services
  • Scheduling platforms
  • Electronic health record or client portal providers
  • Payment processors
  • Email or communication providers
  • Analytics providers; and
  • Other technology or professional service providers.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies, contractual obligations, and applicable law.

When a third party creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on behalf of Brave Horizon Institute and qualifies as a HIPAA business associate, Brave Horizon Institute will address the relationship as required by applicable HIPAA requirements.

9. Payment Information

If online payment functionality is offered, payment information may be processed through a third-party payment processor.

Brave Horizon Institute may not directly receive or store complete payment-card information when payment information is submitted directly to the payment processor.

The privacy and security practices applicable to the payment processor's systems may also be governed by that provider's terms and privacy policies.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to websites, resources, social-media platforms, scheduling systems, or services operated by third parties.

Brave Horizon Institute does not control the privacy, security, content, or practices of third-party websites.

When you leave our website, the privacy policies and terms of the third-party website or service apply. We encourage you to review those policies before providing personal information.

11. Social Media

Brave Horizon Institute may maintain professional social-media accounts or provide links to social-media platforms.

Your interaction with a social-media platform is governed by that platform's privacy practices.

Following, liking, commenting on, sharing, or otherwise interacting with Brave Horizon Institute on social media may be visible to other people and could reveal or suggest an association with a mental health practice.

For your privacy, please do not use public comments or social-media messages to communicate confidential clinical information.

12. Data Security

Brave Horizon Institute uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information under our control.

However, no website, internet transmission, electronic storage system, or security measure can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for taking reasonable precautions when using the internet, including protecting your devices, passwords, accounts, and network connections.

13. Data Retention

Information collected through the website will be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, meet operational needs, resolve disputes, maintain security, or satisfy applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, or professional obligations.

Information that becomes part of a client's health or clinical record may be subject to different retention requirements.

14. Children'S Privacy

Brave Horizon Institute's psychotherapy services are intended for adults.

This website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children through general website features.

If we learn that information has been submitted by a child in a manner that should not have occurred, we will take reasonable steps to address the information consistent with applicable law.

15. Sale Of Personal Information

Brave Horizon Institute does not sell your personal information or Protected Health Information for monetary compensation.

We do not sell PHI.

16. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the information involved and applicable law, you may contact Brave Horizon Institute to:

  • Ask questions about information you have submitted through the website
  • Request correction of inaccurate website contact information
  • Request that we stop certain non-essential communications; or
  • Ask questions about our privacy practices.

Rights involving PHI are described separately in our Notice of Privacy Practices.

17. Changes To This Website Privacy Notice

We may update this Website Privacy Notice periodically to reflect changes in our website, technology, business practices, services, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the revised notice will be posted on this website and the “Last Updated” date will be changed.

We encourage visitors to review this notice periodically.

18. Emergencies

This website is not an emergency or crisis service and is not monitored continuously.

If you are experiencing an immediate medical or mental health emergency, believe you may harm yourself or another person, or are in immediate danger, call 911, go to the nearest emergency department, or call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

Do not submit an emergency or urgent request through a website contact form, email, or other routine electronic communication.

If you have questions about this Website Privacy Notice or Brave Horizon Institute's website privacy practices, please contact:
