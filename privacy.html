Website Privacy Notice

This website publishes general information, educational resources, and information about Brave Horizon Institute. It is not intended to function as a patient portal or electronic health record.

Clinical information

Do not submit diagnoses, symptoms, treatment details, insurance information, appointment history, or other confidential health information through public website forms or ordinary email.

Appointments

Brave Horizon Institute currently provides therapy exclusively through secure telehealth appointments and does not offer in-person therapy at this time. Therapy scheduling and clinical communication should occur through the practice’s approved secure platform.

Website administration

The administrator system included with this site is only for public-facing website content such as articles, videos, links, and selected text. It must not be used for patient or prospective-patient information.

Emergency communication

This site is not monitored for emergencies. If you are in immediate danger or need emergency assistance, call 911. For mental health, suicide, or substance-use crisis support, call or text 988. Call 988

This website privacy notice is a technical starter and should be reviewed by qualified legal/privacy professionals before launch.
