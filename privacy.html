Website Privacy Notice

This website publishes general information and educational resources. It is not intended to collect medical records, diagnoses, treatment information, insurance information, or other protected health information.

Appointments

Appointment requests should be completed through the practice’s approved secure scheduling platform or by calling the office. Do not include private medical or mental-health information in ordinary email.

Administrator system

The administrator page manages only public website content. It is not a patient portal and must not be used for clinical or identifying patient information.

Emergency notice

This website and its email address are not monitored for emergencies. Call 911 or use the appropriate local emergency or crisis service when immediate help is needed.

This starter notice should be reviewed by qualified legal and privacy professionals before publication.
