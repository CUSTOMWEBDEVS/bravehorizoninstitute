Brave Horizon Therapy

From surviving the past to courageously creating the future.

Trauma-informed psychotherapy focused on restoring safety, rebuilding self-trust, reclaiming identity, and expanding what feels possible.

Telehealth therapy only. Brave Horizon Institute currently provides individual therapy exclusively through secure telehealth appointments. In-person therapy is not available at this time.

Who we support

Care for complex relational and trauma-related experiences.

Brave Horizon’s primary clinical focus includes adults navigating the effects of domestic violence, narcissistic abuse, emotional or psychological abuse, coercive control, trauma bonding, high-conflict relationships, family-of-origin trauma, and related PTSD or trauma symptoms.

Treatment approach

Evidence-based care without a one-size-fits-all formula.

Approaches are selected according to the individual’s needs, history, comfort, goals, and clinical appropriateness.

EMDR

Used to support the processing of traumatic experiences and reduce the continued emotional impact of painful memories.

CBT-Informed Strategies

Identify and challenge beliefs and thinking patterns shaped by trauma and prolonged stress.

Mindfulness & Grounding

Strengthen present-moment awareness, emotional regulation, and nervous-system awareness.

Psychoeducation

Replace confusion and self-blame with clearer understanding of trauma and relationship dynamics.

Strength-Based Care

Recognize capabilities, values, resilience, and identity that trauma may have caused someone to overlook.

Trauma-Informed Care

Keep safety, collaboration, choice, empowerment, and respect central throughout the process.
