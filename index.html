Areas of support

Care built around the whole person.

Every person’s history and needs are different. Treatment is tailored to the individual rather than forced into a one-size-fits-all plan.

Trauma

Support for distressing experiences, painful memories, avoidance, and the effects of living in survival mode.

Anxiety

Practical and therapeutic support for chronic worry, fear, stress, and emotional overwhelm.

Grief & Loss

A compassionate space to process change, loss, and the emotions that follow.

Self-Worth

Emotional Regulation

Adolescents & Adults

Individual counseling designed around age, life stage, goals, and personal circumstances.

Treatment approach

Thoughtful care, grounded in evidence.

Treatment methods are selected according to each client’s needs, comfort, history, and goals.

01

EMDR

A structured therapeutic approach commonly used to help people process distressing experiences.

02

Cognitive & Behavioral

Identify patterns between thoughts, feelings, behaviors, and daily experiences.

03

Build awareness of the nervous system, physical responses, and emotional patterns.
